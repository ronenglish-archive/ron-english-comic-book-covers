Comic Book Covers

This series reimagines Ron English's own paintings, drawings, and characters as comic book covers. Existing works from across the catalogue provide the visual and conceptual source material, while Ron directs the titles, cover language, references, styling, and final selections. AI is used as part of the image-making process, allowing individual works and recurring characters from Ron's catalogue to be recast within a fictional comic-book publishing world.
